Privacy policy

Leaf · Publisher: Merdan Turan · Effective date: 26 October 2026

For privacy questions and rights requests, contact leaf.game.support@gmail.com.

Accounts and local game data

Leaf does not require an account. Level progress, stars, best completion times, remaining lives, audio preferences and life recovery times are stored in the application's local storage on your device. Local backup or repair copies may be kept to protect against corrupted saves. Leaf has no server-side account storage or cross-device cloud sync. Depending on your device settings, the operating system may include application data in system backups.

Advertising and Google services

Leaf uses Google AdMob banner and optional rewarded advertisements, and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for required privacy choices. Leaf requests non-personalized advertisements; publisher advertising personalization and publisher first-party ID are disabled. These settings do not mean that no technical data is processed.

According to Google's SDK disclosures, processed data may include IP address and approximate location, device/application information and identifiers, diagnostic/performance information and advertising interactions. The scope depends on the SDK version, permissions and actual delivery settings. This data may be used for advertising delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention. UMP manages privacy preferences.

For the provider's processing and retention practices, see the Google Privacy Policy, Google's use of data from partner services and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures.

Data protection by third parties

We require advertising networks, SDK providers and other service providers that access user data through Leaf or receive data shared by us to provide the same or equivalent level of data protection described in this policy and required by Apple's App Review Guidelines. This obligation includes using data for disclosed purposes, protecting its security, respecting applicable permissions, and fulfilling applicable deletion and other privacy rights.

Data processing responsibilities for Google's advertising services are set out in the Google Ads Data Protection Terms applicable to the service and region, and in Google's privacy disclosures linked above. Linking to a provider's own disclosures does not remove Leaf's data protection responsibilities under this policy. Contact us with questions about these protections or your rights.

Your privacy choices

In regions where required, a privacy form is displayed at startup. Advertising is not started before UMP permits ad requests. Where required by UMP, you can reopen privacy preferences in the game's Settings menu to change your choices or withdraw consent. This does not automatically delete all previously collected data.

Watching advertisements is optional. Refusing consent or unavailable advertisements do not permanently lock the game: missing lives recover every 10 minutes, including time spent outside the game, up to a maximum of three lives. Completing a rewarded advertisement grants one life; clicking an advertisement or installing another application is not required.

Support messages

If you send an email, your email address, message and any information you choose to include are used to handle your request; messages pass through email service providers. You do not need to send passwords, identity documents or payment information. Screenshots are optional; hide personal information before attaching them. Support correspondence is retained for as long as necessary to handle the request, follow up on its resolution and meet applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion through our contact address; any legal retention requirements will also be considered.

Deletion and rights requests

Uninstalling the application normally removes local game data. Operating system backups have separate settings, and restoring a backup may restore data. We cannot remotely access your local save. Data processed by Google may require Google's relevant tools or request procedures; uninstalling Leaf does not automatically delete all data held by Google.

You can contact us to exercise rights such as access, correction, deletion or objection under the law applicable where you live. Only information necessary to assess the request will be requested. Advertising and email service providers may process data in countries outside your country of residence. Their transfer and protection practices are described in their own privacy disclosures. Contact us for information about support requests handled by Leaf and your privacy rights.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. No analytics or tracking scripts have been added to these pages. GitHub may process data such as IP addresses and technical request information to provide and secure its hosting service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
