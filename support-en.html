Support

For help with Leaf, a privacy request or a report about an inappropriate advertisement, contact leaf.game.support@gmail.com.

When describing a problem

  • Include the app version, device model and operating system version.
  • Tell us the level and the steps that led to the problem.
  • For an advertisement report, you can include the approximate time, advertiser or product name, and whether it was a banner or rewarded advertisement.

Do not send passwords, identity documents or payment information. Screenshots are optional; hide personal information before attaching one. You do not need to click a live advertisement to find or test it again.

Lives and advertisements

You can hold up to three lives. Missing lives recover every 10 minutes, including time spent outside the game. Completing an eligible rewarded advertisement grants one life. If it closes early, does not load or is not permitted, you can wait for natural recovery.

Your game save

Progress is stored on your device. Do not uninstall the game as the first step when investigating a save problem; doing so can delete local progress. Leaf does not offer account-based recovery or its own cloud-save service. Operating system backups depend on your device settings; recovery cannot be guaranteed in every case.

Privacy and notices

Where required, you can reopen privacy choices from the game's Settings menu. That menu also includes advertisement reporting and third-party licence notices. Read the privacy policy for details. Reporting an advertisement does not guarantee immediate removal or a specific response from the provider.
